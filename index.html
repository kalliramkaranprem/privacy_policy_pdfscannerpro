Privacy Policy for PDFScannerPro

Last updated: November 2, 2026

PDFScannerPro ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Camera Permissions: We require camera access solely to enable the document scanning functionality. We do not store or transmit your images to any external servers.

User-Provided Data: Any documents you scan, edit, or process are stored locally on your device in private app storage. If you choose to use Firebase authentication (via Google Sign-In) and Firestore for syncing your data, that information is stored securely in your private Firebase project.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the purpose of providing and improving the features of the app, such as scanning, text recognition (OCR), and file management. We do not use your data for advertising, tracking, or sharing with third parties.

3. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data. We do not upload your scanned documents or extracted OCR text to our servers. All processing is done locally on your device or within your authenticated Firebase environment.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
